About

01 · PROFILE

I help organizations keep their SAP landscapes both usable and controlled — building role designs that give people exactly the access their job requires, and no more. That means translating business process into authorization objects, and translating audit and compliance requirements into rule sets that actually hold up under review.

My day-to-day sits across GRC Access Control (risk analysis, emergency access, role maintenance) and native S/4HANA and Fiori security — the two layers have to agree with each other, or the controls don't mean anything.
